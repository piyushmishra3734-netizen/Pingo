rain.mp3

"Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir — https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No attribution is required;
this file exists so that the provenance of an asset in the repository is
never a question somebody has to research.

Chosen from the four loops in that pack by measuring them rather than by
listening: this one has the most high-frequency content and the least
low-frequency rumble, which is the difference between light rain and a
downpour, and it is the steadiest and the longest, which is what keeps a
loop from announcing itself.
